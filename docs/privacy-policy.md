# Privacy Policy for Walkable

**Last updated: 17 October 2026**

Here's the short version: **Walkable keeps your walks on your phone. Nothing is
sent to me. There are no accounts, no tracking, and I don't run any servers.**
The one thing that does go online is the map background, which is downloaded
from a map provider — more on that below.

That's really the whole story, but here are the details.

## What the app uses

While you're recording a walk, Walkable uses your phone's GPS to trace your
route and work out the distance. It can keep recording while your screen is off
or the app is in the background — but only during a walk you've started, and it
stops when you end the walk.

Your walks never leave your phone. They live in the app's private storage on
your device. I can't see them. Nobody can, except you.

No analytics, no ads, no crash reporting, no cloud, no selling anything to
anyone.

## The map

The map behind your route — the streets, parks and water — isn't built into the
app. It's downloaded as small image tiles from [CARTO](https://carto.com), a map
provider, whenever a map is on screen (the app keeps recently seen tiles for a
while, so not every time). Like any web page your phone loads, each of those
requests tells CARTO:

- your phone's IP address, and
- which part of the map is being shown — the area and zoom level. On the walk
  screen that's usually the area around you.

That's all it gets. Your walks, your route and your GPS position are never sent:
the route is drawn on top of the map, on your phone. What CARTO does with those
requests is covered by [CARTO's privacy policy](https://carto.com/privacy).

The map data itself is © OpenStreetMap contributors, and the map style is
© CARTO.

## The location permission

Walkable needs location access (including while the screen is locked) to record
your route. You can turn it on or off anytime in your phone's settings — without
it, walk tracking won't work.

## Your data, your control

- Your walks stay on your phone until you delete them in the app.
- Uninstalling the app deletes everything.

## Kids

Walkable isn't aimed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect
anything from them. (Then again, I don't collect anything from anyone.)

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, I'll update it here and bump the date at the top.
